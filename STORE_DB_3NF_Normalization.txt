3. NORMAL FORM JUDGMENT
-----------------------

FIRST NORMAL FORM (1NF):
- All attributes are atomic
- No repeating groups
Result: All relations are in 1NF

SECOND NORMAL FORM (2NF):
- No partial dependency on composite keys
Result: All relations are in 2NF

THIRD NORMAL FORM (3NF):
- No transitive dependency
- All non-key attributes depend only on candidate keys
Result: All relations are in 3NF


4. REQUIRED DECOMPOSITION
-------------------------
No decomposition is required.
The schema already satisfies 3NF.


5. FINAL CONCLUSION
--------------------
The given STORE_DB schema is fully normalized up to Third Normal Form (3NF).
It avoids redundancy, update anomalies, and dependency violations.
